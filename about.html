Our Services

What We Do

Interior Design

Step into a world where thoughtful space planning meets weatherproof durability. We craft UPVC, PVC, and WPC interiors blending elegance, storage efficiency, and low-maintenance comfort tailored to your family.

Architecture & Modular Craft

At the intersection of functionality and finish, we build structural UPVC doors, sound-insulated windows, full-height wardrobes, and kitchens engineered to resist humidity and termites for decades.

Lighting Design — The Soul of Interior

Lighting shapes atmosphere and brings architectural surfaces alive. From warm false ceiling coves to recessed spotlights inside showcases and pooja mandirs, we layer lighting to elevate comfort and charm.
